FACILITY GUIDELINES

Terms of Service

Last Updated: October 2026 • Kot Murad Khan, Kasur

1. Acceptance of Terms

By visiting, inquiring with, or training at Abu Huraira World Gym Fitness Center (Kot Murad Khan, Kasur), members and visitors agree to abide by these guidelines and safety policies designed for a clean, respectful, and safe athletic environment.

2. Payments & Fee Policy

  • Cash Payments Only: All membership packages, walk-in sessions, and fee payments are strictly payable in cash at the reception. Digital cards or checks are not accepted.
  • Inquiries: Current fee schedules and membership tiers can be confirmed in person at reception or by inquiring via WhatsApp (+92 321 4670428).

3. Facility Use & Etiquette

  • Apparatus & Weights: Members must re-rack weight plates, dumbbells, and return resistance attachments after use.
  • Open-Air Courtyard & Indoor Spaces: Please maintain cleanliness in all workout zones. Spills must be reported immediately.
  • Guidance & Coaching: Training guidance is provided under Ustaad Abu Huraira (Ex Mr. Kasur). Members are encouraged to seek instruction for unfamiliar machines.
  • Timings: The gym is open daily and closes at 12 AM. Please conclude your training routines with sufficient time before closing.
  • Women's Training Hours: Dedicated training slots for women are available. Inquire via WhatsApp for current timing schedules.

4. Health, Safety & Liability

Physical exercise involves inherent physical exertion. Members should ensure they are in suitable physical condition before starting an intensive training regimen. The gym provides maintained equipment, but members exercise at their own responsibility.

5. Inquiries & Assistance

For membership questions, equipment guidance, or timing clarifications:

  • Location: Nizam Pura Rd, near Jamal Market, near City Hospital, Kot Murad Khan, Kasur
  • Phone / WhatsApp: +92 321 4670428
  • Timings: Open daily, closes 12 AM
